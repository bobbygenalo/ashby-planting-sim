# Ashby Planting Sim

Browser-based 3D planting and hardscape simulator for one residential lot. Build brief: `Ashby Yard Planner Requirements.md` (requirement IDs like SITE-1, SUN-4 refer to it).

## Status
- M1 (site model) and M2 (sunlight) are built.
- M3 (plants and growth) is built: growth curve, 57-species library (26 rows come from the owner's plant data sheet of 2026-11-21, each marked Recall only or Partly verified in `sheet`; the rest are unverified, low confidence), custom species form, palette select/deselect, placement with drag-to-move, inspector, timeline to year 30, plant forms by habit, mature-width ring, plant shadows in the sun map, leaf-off shade and drawing for deciduous plants, bloom color on plants, sun-need mismatch flags. Bloom calendar and pollinator summary are M5. Custom species are not persisted until M7.
- M4 (hardscape) is built: draw, reshape (corner handles), move and delete patios, paths, beds, lawn, decks, gravel, raised beds, walls and fences; 13-material library; takeoff with CSV; permeable vs impervious per zone; easement, root-zone and driveway warnings; snapping to corners, edges and grid; live dimensions; shade from walls, fences, decks and raised beds; undo/redo (Ctrl/Cmd+Z) across plants, palette and hardscape. Existing driveway can be removed per scenario. Not done: neighbor context (SITE-7), slope (SITE-8), raised-bed soil fill volume (WS-14).
- M5 (water and soil) is built: MWELO-method water demand per plant, hydrozone, yard zone and lot with a monthly chart and weekly watering figures; hydrozones with irrigation types and mixed-category warnings; establishment multipliers; optional effective rainfall; informational water budget; per-zone soil profiles with plant soil-fit flags; planting hole, excavation, amendment and mulch quantities; raised bed fill; tree rootable soil check; bloom calendar with gap flags and gap suggestions; pollinator summary and warnings (invasive, bee-attracting groundcover in a play area); plant schedule CSV; assumptions panel.
- **The monthly ETo values in `src/data/water.json` are a placeholder** (the official MWELO Appendix A row for Los Angeles could not be retrieved). Replace the twelve values and set `eto.status` to `verified`; the banner and the assumptions panel update from that field. Verified: the ETWU formula and 0.62, plant factor ranges, drip 0.81 and spray 0.75 efficiency, residential ETAF 0.55, Downtown LA 1991-2020 rainfall normals. Unverified: species water categories and soil needs, establishment multipliers, hand-watering efficiency, effective rainfall share, root ball sizes, tree soil target.
- M6 (conflicts and suggestions) is built: a monthly scan over 30 years reports the first year each conflict appears (canopy overlap, canopy touching a building or roof, trunk too close to a foundation, paving or the lot line, height past the easement cap), with red/amber rings in the yard and a Layout tab. The placement finder scores a 1 ft grid in a yard for one species on sun fit, clearance at mature size and hydrozone match with adjustable weights, shows a heatmap and the top five spots, and explains each score; placing a plant at a top spot yields no conflicts (tested). Equal scores break toward the cell with the most room.
- Conflict distances (`conflicts` and `suggest` in `engine.json`) and species root clearances (`roots` in `species.json`) are guidance, unverified. Conflicts from the placeholder site trees are listed separately.
- M7 (scenarios and sharing) is built: named scenarios (create, rename, duplicate, delete, switch), autosave in the browser plus JSON export/import, read-only share links per scenario (create, update to today's plan, turn off, blur the location), side-by-side comparison, and a printable plan sheet at 1 in = 20 ft.
- Sharing runs on a file-backed share API served by the Vite dev/preview server (`server/`, snapshots in `.share-data/`, git-ignored). Hosting is still undecided: `docs/sharing-api.md` is the contract a hosted version (for example a Cloudflare Worker over KV) must keep. The app bundle you host still contains `src/data/site.json`; blurring the location removes it from shared data only.
- **Site corrections from the owner's images:** the ADU is modeled from the owner's floor and roof plans as three connected rectangles (`adu-bedroom`, `adu-link`, `adu-great`, group `adu`): about 566 sq ft of footprint, which with the 12'-2 3/4" by 4'-0" entry landing is the printed 616 sq ft. Coordinates come from the plan's grid dimensions (wall faces sit 2 7/8" outside the grid, 8'-11 1/2" on the west) and reproduce three numbers printed on the plot plan exactly. Roofs follow the roof plan: two shed roofs falling to the west (bedroom 10'-5" to 13'-1 1/4", great room 9'-4" to 13'-1 1/8", above finished floor, plus 0.8 ft for the floor above grade) and a flat roof over the bath and hall. The open yard notch (southwest) and courtyard (northeast) are open ground. The mature ficus is one tree between the garage and the ADU: its trunk (10.9, 116.4) was marked by the owner on a screenshot of the plan (good to about 1 ft); its canopy (28 ft across, 26 ft tall) comes from a photo and is assumed centered on the trunk.
- The ADU's gutter and overhang edges are approximated by its wall faces, except the 2'-5 1/4" south overhang.
- The ADU's front facades are recessed 2'-5 1/4" between side walls that run to the ground (parts `adu-fin-*`, group `adu-fins`, 0.5 ft thick), and the shed roof continues over the recess as a 0.6 ft slab open underneath (`roofThickness`, `overhangPoints`, shadow body `<id>:slab`). The great room's fins sit on its east and west walls; the bedroom wing's projection (2'-5 1/4") and its single west fin are assumed to match, because its recess dimensions are not read from the plans. Slab thickness is a placeholder.
- **The house** is six parts (group `house`) built from the owner's roof plan and four elevations: a main east-west gable (ridge at the 13'-10 5/8" top of roof), a front gable over the east half of the south front (north-south ridge meeting the main ridge), a lower porch shed roof over the west half, a small west gable wing (ridge 11'-5 3/4"), and a rear wing in two parts (ridge 11'-2 7/8") with a 7'-7" by 7'-9 1/4" cut-out at the northwest corner facing the garage. Heights are above natural grade; the ground floor is 1'-3" above it, and the model's terrain is flat though the real grade rises about half a foot front to back. The house is 41.1 ft deep (grid 1 to grid 5; front wall about 25.0 ft from the street). Side walls are placed from the roof edge being about 2.5 ft from the east lot line (good to about 0.5 ft); the middle grid line B, and so the front gable's position, is good to about 0.6 ft. Conflicts and clearances treat a building of several parts as one (`groupName`). Fence heights and the garage eave height are still placeholders.
- The app is deployed to GitHub Pages by `.github/workflows/deploy.yml` (base path `/ashby-planting-sim/` through `VITE_BASE`). `worker/` is a Cloudflare Worker with KV that serves share links and a sync-key-protected workspace store for cloud backup (`src/share/cloud.ts`, `src/store/cloudSync.ts`); set the repo variable `API_BASE` to turn both on in the Pages build. Visitors are never asked about backup: the first edit gives a browser its own key, edits save online automatically, and Create link saves first. The Scenarios tab keeps the key under "Use on another device". Every scenario row has its own share links (create, copy, update, turn off); the "Blur the location" checkbox applies to new links. Setup is in `docs/cloud-deploy.md`. Without `API_BASE` the deployed app has no share links or cloud backup.

## Commands
- In dev, the store is on `window.__app` for inspecting state from the browser console.
- The Browser pane must be visible for the 3D canvas to repaint. A hidden pane runs the page as a background tab and screenshots go stale.
- `npm run dev` runs the app. Add `?mode=view` to the URL for read-only view mode.
- `npm test` runs Vitest. `npm run typecheck` runs tsc. `npm run build` does both checks plus the production build.

## Architecture rules (from the brief)
- `src/engine/` is pure functions with no React or three.js imports. Calculations live here and are unit tested.
- `src/data/site.json` is the only place site values live. `src/data/engine.json` holds every coefficient and default. Do not inline numbers in code.
- Scene and UI read config, reference data and the store. Changing `site.json` alone retargets the app.
- View mode blocks plan mutations in the store (`mutate` guard in `src/store/store.ts`), not only in the UI. Viewer controls (camera, time, layers) stay allowed.

## Conventions
- Site coordinates are decimal feet: X across the lot from the driveway side, Y from the street toward the rear, Z up. three.js gets (x, z, -y) through one rotated group in `SiteScene.tsx`.
- `rearBearingDeg` is the compass bearing of the +Y axis. `driveSide` says which side the driveway is on facing the house from the street. Both are unverified assumptions.
- Printed plan dimensions are listed in `site.json` under `printed`, each with a measure rule. `checkPrintedDimensions` verifies them to 1 inch, and the Plan check panel shows the result.
- Plants: `engine/growth.ts` (size curve, shadow solids, sun-fit), `engine/library.ts` (filters, palette). Species data is `src/data/species.json`; every coefficient is in `engine.json` under `growth` and `timeline`.
- SUN-6 fit uses a growing-season (Mar-Oct) sun map computed on the site without plants, so a plant is not judged by its own shade. The displayed map includes plant shade at the timeline year.
- Sun model is clear-sky geometry (NOAA algorithm in `solar.ts`). Sun-hours rasterizes shadow hulls per time step in a web worker.

## Known gaps
- Fence heights, the garage eave height (9.3 ft, a 4:12 roof) and tree positions are placeholders flagged in `site.json`.
- Convex footprints only for shadow casting. Use several structures for L-shapes until parts are supported.

## Layout (UI)
- Top bar: site, scenario switcher (New, Manage, Share open the scenarios dialog), save status, undo/redo. The scenario is the container for everything, so it leads.
- Right panel has two levels: Design (Plants, Hardscape), Analyze (Layout, Water & soil, Pollinators, Sun hours), Outputs (plan sheet, schedules, model check, assumptions). Whatever is selected in the yard, plant or hardscape, opens in a `SelectionCard` over the yard against its right edge, just left of the panel, with its bottom 10 px above the SimDock (it tracks the dock's height), on any tab.
- Over the yard: view buttons at top left, Layers at top right; one `SimDock` at the bottom for growth and sun.
- The plant `palette` is only a star shortlist (any plant can be placed); it never hides placed plants.
- Shuffle (top bar button opens a vertical bar down the left of the yard, below the view buttons, so the scene stays visible; the two modes are horizontal tabs and the options beneath are checkboxes): `engine/shuffle.ts` explores variations of new work only (existing plants and hardscape never change): look-alike species swaps (same type, sun and water, size within a band), short plant nudges off buildings and hard cover, same-family hardscape materials, palette colors, and a random growth year plus sun date and time. It is seeded and repeatable; every number lives in `engine.json` under `shuffle`. The store action `shuffle` puts the result in a new scenario by default ("Shuffle again" replaces that copy until "Keep this one"), or changes the scenario in place with undo.
- Shuffle has two modes. "Scenario data" tweaks new work (with an optional "Starred only" limit on species swaps). "New concept" (`engine/concept.ts`, config in `engine.json` under `concept`) builds a fresh patio, garden path, beds and plants from nothing in a new scenario, copying only the starred palette, which plants are drawn from when "Starred only" is on. Concepts keep to one water-use family, respect root clearance, room to grow and each plant's light need (see smart placement below).
- Fruit: edible fruiting species carry a `fruit` block in `species.json` (shape, size, ripe and unripe colors and months, count). `engine/fruit.ts` decides when it shows (plant at least `fruit.minSizeFraction` of mature size, in season for the sun date's month, more of it as the plant grows, ripe fruit stays on bare deciduous trees) and where it hangs on the canopy; `scene/Plants.tsx` draws it as instanced spheres at `fruit.displayScale` times real size, behind the "Fruit on trees" layer. Months, sizes and colors are general estimates for display.
- Plant shapes: `scene/forms.ts` turns a habit, size and trunk share into parts (ellipsoid, dome, lathe, cone, cylinder, box), and `engine/canopy.ts` holds the canopy outline the shapes and the fruit share. `tests/plantforms.test.ts` measures every species at every growth stage (container and year) and fails if a shape goes under the ground, is taller or wider than its data, floats off its trunk, or if fruit hangs off the canopy. Open the app with `?lab` (development only) to see species side by side at several ages: `?lab&species=cercis-occidentalis,malus-mutsu` or a habit name or `fruit` or `all`, plus `years=0,3,10,20`, `month=1..12`, `spacing=feet`, `pitch=0.1` for a side-on view.
- The garage and driveway come from the owner's garage plan: walls 18'-2" by 20'-1" with the west wall 2'-11" from the west lot line, a 20'-2" by 22'-1" gable roof (1'-0" overhang, ridge front to back, ridge 12'-8" printed), and a driveway that starts 1'-3" in from the lot line with a 16'-0" apron for 10'-0" and a taper out to 26'-0" from the garage front wall. The garage rear wall is placed from the printed 19'-10 3/4" to the ADU and assumes that is wall to wall (about 1 ft of uncertainty); the narrow part of the driveway (about 7.5 ft) is estimated from the taper's slope. A small pad on the north side of the garage and a thin dashed line near the west side of the plan are not modeled. Existing hardscape always comes from `site.json` (`syncExistingHardscape`), so a saved scenario never keeps an old driveway shape.
- Life layer ("People, dogs and bees" in Layers, on by default with 3 people, 2 dogs, the hive and the bees): `engine/life.ts` is a pure simulation. A 1 ft walking grid blocks buildings, plant canopies (with a margin wider than half a cell, so nobody can stand in one), raised beds, walls, the lot edge and the hive, and gives each kind of ground a cost: paths are cheapest, then paving, lawn, open ground, gravel, mulch and beds. People pick destinations (about half on paths, the rest anywhere) and take the cheapest route by A*, so they gravitate to paths without being limited to them. Dogs (at most 2) trot after a person and sniff about; people at most 4. Bees (26) leave a hive of four stacked boxes, which sits well away from paths and buildings and toward the back of the lot, fly to flowers in bloom this month that bees visit, feed, and return; with nothing in bloom they stay near the hive. People and dogs keep 4.5 ft from the hive. `scene/Life.tsx` draws it; in development the simulation is on `window.__life`. Every number is in `engine.json` under `life`.
- Naming: the app is "Ashby Planting Sim". The site data holds no street address, and share links can blur the location (drop the site name, round the coordinates to 0.1 degree). Internal names (`ashby-planner/...` storage keys, the `ashby-planner-api` Worker) are unchanged so saved work and the deployed Worker keep working.
- In-canvas controls: the camera views are one row of buttons at the top left of the yard, switched with the number keys 1 to 6 (`ui/shortcuts.ts`; ignored while typing, with Cmd/Ctrl/Alt/Shift, or with a dialog open); Layers is at the top right (the Measure and Snap buttons were removed; the measure tool and snap grid still exist in the store). Bees are drawn one unit long with a dark head, a yellow abdomen and two thin black stripes (`beeGeometry` in `scene/forms.ts`).
- The cast and the doors: up to 15 people join in a fixed mixed order from `life.people.roster` in `engine.json`: ten adults, two 9-year-olds, an 11-year-old, a 14-year-old and a 3-year-old, each with an age, height and walking speed. Children are drawn with child proportions (`personProportions` in `scene/forms.ts`: bigger heads, shorter legs), and the 3-year-old stays near an adult (like dogs near a person). `doors` in `site.json` (house front and back, garage, ADU; positions assumed, flagged `estimate`) are where people go in: about one trip in five heads for a building, picks a building at random, walks to the open ground outside its door, steps in, is hidden for a random 10 to 60 seconds, and steps back out. Dogs and the toddler wait outside. A site with no `doors` keeps everyone outdoors; a building not in the plan (the ADU switched off) loses its door.
- Shuffle's "Starred only" chips (for a new concept and for species swaps) start off: plants come from the whole library until the owner limits them to the starred palette.
- Retired species: the pineapple guava was replaced by a kumquat and a satsuma mandarin. `RETIRED_SPECIES` in `engine/scenario.ts` turns a plant of a retired species into its replacement (guava to kumquat) when a scenario loads, is imported or is opened from a share link, with a warning, so older work keeps its plants.
- Leafless deciduous plants (`isLeafOff`) are drawn as opaque bare branches, not a ghost canopy: `engine/branches.ts` (`branchSkeleton`, config `branches` in `engine.json`) grows limbs that fork twice from the top of the trunk, every tip inside `canopyRadius`, deterministic by plant id; `scene/branchGeometry.ts` merges them into one mesh. Fruit on a bare tree hangs from the twig tips (`hangingSpots` in `scene/Plants.tsx`). Only persimmon keeps ripe fruit through its leaf-off months; apples are bare Dec-Feb so their Aug-Nov fruit always has leaves (`tests/branches.test.ts`).
- Concept paths (`engine/pathnet.ts`, config `concept.path` in `engine.json`) connect places instead of wandering off a random edge: early (before beds and plants take the room) they link the patio, every door (the garage is skipped; it opens onto the driveway), a walk from the front door to the street, and any lawn or deck; late (after planting) they reach up to `maxFruitHubs` fruit trees, stopping `fruitStandFt` past the mature radius. Routes are cheapest-path (Dijkstra on a 1 ft grid, 2 ft from buildings, areas and plant canopies), grow like a spanning tree so places share routes and end on earlier paths, and a place cut off from the network (the front yard) starts its own. The theme's old `paths` count is gone; `path.max` caps the total. Plants keep `plantEdgeFt` off a path and `doorKeepClearFt` off a door. Path names say what they join. Tests: `tests/pathnet.test.ts`.
- Decks, walls and fences are no longer drawn or generated: `hardscape.notDrawable` in `engine.json` hides them from the Draw buttons and every theme's `deck` chance is 0. The types, materials and rendering stay so older saved plans still load.
- Raised-bed crops: each raised bed grows a cool-season crop (Nov-Apr) and a warm-season crop (May-Oct) from `src/data/crops.json` (14 crops: lettuce, spinach, kale, broccoli, carrots, snap peas, tomatoes, peppers, eggplant, zucchini, cucumbers, green beans, basil, strawberries; months are general estimates, unverified). `engine/crops.ts` is pure: `cropStage` (sown as a seedling, grows to harvest, spent in its last month, pulled, bare until the next sowing; strawberries are cut back Dec-Jan and regrow), `activeCrop`, `defaultCropPlan` (picked from the bed id, so beds differ), `cropPositions` (grid at the crop's spacing inside the bed). `scene/Crops.tsx` draws instanced plants and produce on the bed top by the sun date's month, behind the "Raised-bed crops" layer; the bed's inspector picks the two crops (`crops` on the element). Crops are not yet in the water, soil or schedule outputs. Tests: `tests/crops.test.ts`.
- Playing growth also advances the sun date by `timeline.daysPerStep` (365/12 days) per month of growth, wrapping the year, so leaves, fruit and the raised-bed crops follow the seasons while the plants grow. Moving the date by hand during play is respected.
- Smart placement (`engine/placement.ts`): concepts and shuffles are given a growing-season sun map (`buildSunMap`, buildings only, about 1 s, cached per site in the store as `sunMapFor`) and keep to three rules. Light: a plant goes only where `sunFit` is `ok` (no sun lover in shade, no shade plant in full sun); a species that finds no such spot gives way to another draw (`concept.plants.speciesTries`). Room: neighbors stand at least `overlapAllowance` (now 1.0) times the average of their mature widths apart (groundcovers may run under others; `roomRatio`). Trees: a tree's full-size canopy never covers a raised bed (`shadesRaisedBed`), and raised beds already keep off the site's own trees. In a shuffle, species swaps and nudges must keep a plant's spot suitable, and a spot that was already poor is not made worse. Not modeled: shade that placed plants cast on each other, and hand-drawn beds under trees are not warned about. Tests: `tests/smartplacement.test.ts`.
- Keyboard: number keys 1-6 pick views; the space bar plays and pauses growth (`isPlayPauseKey` in `ui/shortcuts.ts`), ignored while typing in a field, with a modifier, on key repeat, or with a dialog open.
- Plan view: the camera fits the whole lot plus a 35 ft margin into the part of the yard the SimDock leaves free, and centers it there (measured from the dock's height when the view is set).
- Top bar: all type is one size (13 px, set by `.topbar *` in styles.css).
- The right panel is a drawer: the icon button at the far right of the top bar shows and hides it (`panelOpen` in `ui/App.tsx`, remembered in localStorage as `ashby-planner/panel-open`). Closed, the yard takes the full width; the selection card stays over the yard.
- Side panel and selection card share one width (`--side-w`, 360 px, in styles.css). The panel drawer animates: the grid column slides to 0 over 0.28 s and the content keeps its width while it slides (no animation in the stacked narrow layout). The open drawer button is a quiet tint, not Share's solid fill. Undo and redo use inline Feather-style stroke icons (`FeatherIcon` in `ui/TopBar.tsx`: corner-up-left and corner-up-right).
- Playing growth loops: at the end of the timeline (year 30) it wraps to today and keeps playing; press play or the space bar to stop.
- Growth stages are in the app, not only in ?lab: a "Growth" button on each library row and "Growth stages…" in a placed plant's card open `GrowthStagesDialog` (store `growthStagesFor`): the species planted and at ages from `engine.growthStages.years`, a month picker (starts at the sun date's month), side view, and a second species to compare, in 3D you can turn. `scene/GrowthStages.tsx` draws it and the ?lab page reuses it.
